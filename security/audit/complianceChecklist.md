# Compliance checklist

## SOC 2 controls

- [ ] Access reviews are completed quarterly and retained for one year.
- [ ] Production changes require peer review and protected-branch checks.
- [ ] Authentication, authorization, and administrative events are immutable and monitored.
- [ ] Backups are encrypted, tested quarterly, and have documented recovery objectives.
- [ ] Security incidents are assigned an owner and tracked through closure.

## GDPR controls

- [ ] Collection purposes and retention periods are documented in the privacy notice.
- [ ] Data export and deletion requests are verified and fulfilled within the statutory period.
- [ ] Consent and marketing preferences are auditable.
- [ ] Processors and international transfers have current agreements.
- [ ] Data minimization and encryption are applied to personal and payment data.

## Release evidence

Record the release identifier, review approvals, scan results, migration checksum, and rollback reference for every production deployment.
